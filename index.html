Essential Tourist Guidelines

Cash in Smaller Notes

Major cards work inside metropolitan malls in Manila and Cebu, but provincial outrigger boats, rural tricycles, and roadside food stalls only transact in local Peso cash (PHP ₱20, ₱50, ₱100 bills).

Accredited Eco-Guides Only

Whether trekking mountains like Pulag, spelunking in Sagada, or chartering bancas in Coron, always employ municipal-certified guides to keep you safe and respect local heritage boundaries.

Reef-Safe & Eco-Friendly

Please use marine-friendly formulations of sunblock in coral lagoons. Bring reusable water flasks as island municipalities strictly minimize single-use plastics.
